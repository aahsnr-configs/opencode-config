# Academic Research

1. Translate the question into a date window, population or domain, inclusion criteria, and exclusion criteria.
2. When connected, use Exa for discovery; Consensus for peer-reviewed literature; alphaXiv for preprint and paper/code exploration; Undermind for deeper discovery. SciSpace is an optional external service, not an automatically transferable ChatGPT plugin.
3. Deduplicate by DOI or canonical URL and verify titles, venue, years, and publication status from original sources.
4. Distinguish preprints, published work, and editorial summaries; assess design, bias, reproducibility, and limitations.
5. Return a concise evidence matrix with DOI or stable URL, methods, findings, applicability, and caveats.
6. Never invent references; if a connected service fails, say so rather than presenting guesses as sourced findings.
